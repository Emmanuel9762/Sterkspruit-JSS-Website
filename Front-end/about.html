School Leadership

Mr TF Slater

Principal

Mr. Slater began his journey at Sterkspruit Junior Secondary School in the early 2000s as a dedicated educator. His commitment to academic excellence and holistic development quickly earned him recognition, leading to his promotion to principal. Under his leadership, the school has significantly advanced its academic and extracurricular programs, establishing itself as a leading institution. Mr. Slater's vision and dedication have created a nurturing, inclusive environment where students are encouraged to excel and grow, making Sterkspruit JSS a beacon of quality education.

Mr VC Madlamba

Deputy Principal

Mr. Madlamba brings decades of educational experience and a passion for student development to his role as Deputy Principal. His innovative approach to curriculum development and teacher mentorship has strengthened our academic programmes. He works closely with faculty to ensure that teaching standards remain high and that every student receives the support they need to succeed. His dedication to the school community is unwavering and inspirational.

Our Dedicated Staff

The dedicated faculty and staff at Sterkspruit Junior Secondary School exemplify commitment to education and student development. This diverse group of educators brings a wealth of knowledge, experience, and passion to the classroom, fostering an environment where students can thrive academically and personally. Their collaborative spirit and unified mission to provide a holistic education are evident in their teamwork and dedication. By continuously improving their teaching methods and supporting each other, they create a nurturing and inspiring atmosphere that encourages students to reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic, inclusive, and excellent education that nurtures each student's intellectual, social, and emotional growth. We are committed to developing critical thinkers, responsible citizens, and lifelong learners who will contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To be the leading junior secondary school in the Eastern Cape, recognized for academic excellence, innovative teaching methods, and the development of well-rounded individuals who are prepared for the challenges of high school and beyond.

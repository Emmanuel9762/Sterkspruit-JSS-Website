Before You Apply

Eligibility & Requirements

Please review the following requirements to ensure your application is complete and eligible for consideration.

Age Requirement

Applicants for Grade 8 must be between 13 and 15 years of age at the start of the academic year.

Previous School Records

A certified copy of the latest school report (Grade 7 for new Grade 8 entrants) is required with your application.

Identity Documents

Certified copies of the learner's birth certificate and the parent or guardian's ID document must be included.

Proof of Residence

A utility bill or official document confirming your residential address within the school's catchment area.

Application Deadline

Applications for the 2025 academic year must be submitted no later than 31 October 2024.

Transfer Applicants

Learners transferring from another school must provide a transfer letter and clearance from their previous institution.
